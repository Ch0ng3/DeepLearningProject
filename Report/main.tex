\section{Method}
\subsection{Protocol}
All experiments use DomainBed with ERM on PACS, leave-one-domain-out (4 test domains), a 20\% hold-out of every domain, and model selection on the training-domain validation set; the test domain is never used for selection or tuning. Results are the test-domain accuracy of the selected checkpoint, as mean $\pm$ standard deviation over 3 seeds (trial seeds 0--2, identical splits for all methods).
